Ernest Lo

I am a researcher and engineer interested in medical applications of machine learning and AI, My work previously focused on building computational fluid dynamics simulations of bloodflow in imaging-generated 3D models of coronary arteries, as well as simulations of plaque dynamics.

I received my PhD in Medical Physics and Biomedical Engineering from University College London, where I was advised by Professor Ryo Torii and Dr. Leon Menezes. My research explored methods of improving imaging based diagnoses of Coronary Artery Disease, specifically CT-FFR.

I have also worked in the startup ecosystem, including a Y-Combinator funded startup in the healthtech space.
